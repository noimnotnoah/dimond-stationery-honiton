Our Story

Serving Honiton from the same High Street shop since 1888

A Family Tradition

A. Dimond & Co Ltd opened its doors on Honiton High Street in 1888, during the reign of Queen Victoria. What began as a small shop selling writing instruments and paper has grown into Devon's most cherished independent stationer.

Now run by the fourth generation of the Dimond family, we still believe in the personal touch — from recommending the perfect fountain pen to ordering that hard-to-find ink cartridge. Our shelves are stocked with everything the modern office needs, alongside traditional stationery that honours our craft.
